Library Services

Everything you can do at Readers' Haven beyond borrowing a book. Most services are free for members; a few have a small fee, listed below.

Online catalogue

Search the full collection from home, check if a title is on the shelf, and reserve it for pick-up.

Available
24 hours a day, every day
Cost
Free for members

How to reserve a book

  1. Open the catalogue and search by title, author, or subject.
  2. Select a title and choose Reserve.
  3. Sign in with your membership number.
  4. Collect the book from the front desk within 3 days.

E-books and audiobooks

Borrow digital titles on your phone, tablet, or computer. Loans return themselves, so there are no late fees.

Available
24 hours a day, every day
Loan period
14 days, up to 5 titles at a time

How to start reading

  1. Ask the front desk for your digital access code.
  2. Sign in to the e-library with your membership number.
  3. Pick a title and select Borrow.
  4. Download it to read offline.

Study-room booking

Quiet rooms for individual study or small groups of up to six people, with a whiteboard and power sockets.

Available
Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
Booking length
Up to 2 hours per day
Cost
Free for members

How to book a room

  1. Choose a date and time slot at the front desk or on the catalogue page.
  2. Show your membership card to confirm the booking.
  3. Arrive within 10 minutes of your start time or the room is released.

Printing and scanning

Black-and-white and colour printing, photocopying, and scanning to email or USB.

Available
Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM
Cost
Scanning is free. Printing is charged per page.

How to print

  1. Send your file to the library print email or bring it on a USB drive.
  2. Collect the print code from the front desk.
  3. Enter the code at the printer and pay for the pages.

Free Wi-Fi

Fast wireless internet in the reading hall, children's corner, and study rooms for members and visitors.

Available
Whenever the library is open
Cost
Free for everyone

How to connect

  1. Join the ReadersHaven-Guest network.
  2. Open a browser and accept the usage terms.
  3. Browse for up to 3 hours per session, then reconnect if you need more time.

Need help with a service?

Our staff can walk you through any of the steps above.
